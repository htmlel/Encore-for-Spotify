Setting up

Spotify asks every person who uses a small app like this one to register it themselves. It takes about five minutes, once. You need Spotify Premium.

1Create a Spotify app

Open developer.spotify.com in this browser. Tap the menu at the top right and Log in.

Then open the menu again, choose Dashboard, and tap Create app.

  • App name and description: anything you like.
  • Redirect URI: paste exactly this, then tap Add:
  • Under "Which API/SDKs are you planning to use?" pick Web API.
  • Agree to the terms and tap Save.

2Add yourself as a user

In your new app, open User Management and add your name and the email address of your Spotify account.

3Copy your Client ID

On the app's Basic Information page, copy the Client ID.

4Connect the watch app

In the Pebble app, open this watch app's Settings, paste the Client ID, tap Copy login link, and follow the three steps shown there.
